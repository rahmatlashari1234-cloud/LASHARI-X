// 05 — CLIENT POLICIES

HOW WE WORK

Clear, respectful, and professional working standards that keep every project aligned, timely, and stress-free.

POLICY 01

Confidentiality

We treat all brand strategy, internal documents, campaign ideas, and assets as confidential. Information shared in the onboarding process remains protected and used only for the agreed project scope.

POLICY 02

Timelines & Delivery

Project schedules are defined during kickoff and communicated clearly before work begins. Delays caused by incomplete client feedback or late asset delivery may require timeline adjustments, but all milestones are tracked transparently.

POLICY 03

Revision Process

Each project includes a defined revision cycle to support refinement and polish. Major scope changes, new concepts, or expanded deliverables beyond the signed plan are treated as additional work and discussed before implementation.
